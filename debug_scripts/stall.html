What is the difference between AI humanizing, paraphrasing, and rewriting?

This misunderstanding of the meanings of those three concepts is widespread, but, actually, the distinction between them is rather small. Humanization concerns tonality and fluency. The text does not change its meaning and structure; only the word choice is changed until the text stops being mechanical. Paraphrasing is about expressing the same thought in other words. The vocabulary and syntax are changed, but not the thought at all. Just try it out with the help of our Clever Humanizer Paraphrase Tool. Rewriting goes further than both. The rewriter can make changes both in the word choice and in the expression of an idea in a text, and the result will be quite different from the original one. In a nutshell: humanizing is polishing, paraphrasing is retelling, rewriting is rebuilding.
